How TAVLIQO works.

We handle the tech. You focus on the food.

01

We Onboard You

Contact our team. We create your restaurant profile, upload your menu, and hand over your private dashboard link.

02

Print Your QR

Download your custom, unguessable QR code from your dashboard. Place it on tables, counters, or entrance.

03

Customers Scan

No app to download. They scan with their camera, browse the menu, and place an order instantly.

04

Orders appear live on your dashboard. Accept, prepare, and mark as paid all in one place.
